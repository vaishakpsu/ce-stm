The PAX Group at the Institute for Gravitation and the Cosmos (IGC), Penn State University, in collaboration with [insert partner organizations], is proud to organize a 3-day Workshop on the Science Traceability Matrix for Cosmic Explorer (CE). Cosmic Explorer is the next-generation gravitational wave observatory set to revolutionize our understanding of the universe.

This workshop will bring together experts, researchers, and stakeholders to explore the scientific goals, technical challenges, and development roadmap for Cosmic Explorer. Attendees will engage in discussions, presentations, and collaborative sessions aimed at advancing the project’s mission.


## Workshop Details

### Dates: May 19–21, 2025

We are pleased to announce the first CE-ET Mock Data Challenge Workshop, which will be held in person at Penn State’s Institute for Gravitation and the Cosmos. This workshop marks a significant milestone in the development of Cosmic Explorer, focusing on the Science Traceability Matrix and its role in shaping the future of gravitational wave astronomy.


### Location
The workshop will take place at Whitmore Laboratory, located on the beautiful campus of Penn State University.
